PERFORMANCE_RELEVANT DEVIATIONS =
1. Interior-of-damaged-entry reuse impossible (D5): rebuild work grows with the damaged top-level entry's full extent (e.g. a damaged 1000-line list rebuilds all undamaged items) where the donor reuses the undamaged interior. Under-reuses relative to donor — penalizes H3 in any later H3/Horse-A comparison on container-heavy workloads.
2. 3.
