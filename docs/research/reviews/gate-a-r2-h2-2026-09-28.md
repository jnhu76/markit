PERFORMANCE_RELEVANT DEVIATIONS (all qualitative; no timing performed)
1. Mid-container candidate discovery/granularity (D3/D5): donor takes item-level sibling runs inside damaged containers; local cannot (top-level line alignment + blank-line margin). Cost proportional to damaged-container size. Direction: against H2.
2. Per-consult O(top-level-blocks) discovery scan (D11): donor amortized-linear cursor vs local per-consult full entries rebuild + scan; O(B²) worst case in refusal-heavy regimes (definition-changing + has_ref dense). Direction: overhead, payload-dependent dominance.
3. Blank-line take margin (declared): forfeits undamaged runs starting at non-blank-separated boundaries (verified: entire undamaged tight list after a damaged para). Bounded by the forfeited run's size. Against H2.
4. Left-edge continuation margin (declared): can drop the ENTIRE left fragment for edits inside tight containers. Against H2.
5. Open-edge one-block exclusion (declared): whole adjacent block vs donor's ~one line; hurts the large-block axis. Against H2.
6. Right-piece minGap drop (undeclared asymmetry): ≤ 127-byte tails lose reuse donor keeps. Against H2. Bounded.
7. Missing donor guards (NotLast, trailing-partial-line, openEnd−1): local reuses slightly MORE than donor in run-end/document-end shapes (bounded, oracle-safe). For H2.
8. Reference clause + rematerialization (benchmark-necessitated addition): work the donor never does. Against H2 on reference-dense payloads.
Net: the model UNDER-measures the anchor's reuse on container/interruptor/edge shapes — it is not a pro-H2 strawman; it is an anti-H2-biased, soundness-conservative restriction set whose aggregate contradicts the frozen "not a strict subset" claim.

CLAIM BOUNDARY
The defensible claim is: "a lezer-inspired, soundness-conservative fragment-table-gated whole-top-level-block reuse model with structural ContextKey vouching, under the #22 semantic core". NOT defensible as written: R5 §7 FIDELITY_BOUNDARY's "taking runs of sibling blocks at nested levels reproduces Lezer markdown's block-run reuse (the HORSE_BOUNDARY_AMBIGUITIES item 1 shape), not a strict subset" — the implementation cannot take mid-container sibling runs (the operative Lezer shape; empirically 25/30 items upstream vs 0 locally), and "minGap = 128 … the frozen @lezer/common default" is applied symmetrically while @lezer/common applies it only before/between changes. Results attributed to "Lezer-style fragment reuse" on container-damage, tight-boundary, and reference-dense workloads carry an anti-H2 bias relative to the donor mechanism and must be labeled as such.

FIDELITY_VERDICT = MATERIAL_DEVIATION
The fragment-table lifecycle (D1/D2/D6/D7/D8/D10/D12), ContextKey vouching (D4 core), whole-block structural sharing, and per-line natural-degradation cadence are faithful; the reference clause is a declared, benchmark-necessitated adaptation. But two P1 deviations — the absent mid-container block-run reuse that the frozen boundary explicitly claims is reproduced, and the asymptotically heavier per-consult discovery — change dominant-work and reuse-opportunity behavior on frozen payload axes (DeepContainer/large-container, tight boundaries, reference-dense low-reuse regimes). Per the Gate-A rule (report, do not repair), these are reported for a mechanism-freeze decision: either the claim boundary is rewritten to "conservative strict-subset-with-stronger-vouching" and the container-axis results are bounded accordingly, or the cursor gains live-level candidate discovery before any H2/Horse-A comparative claim about fragment reuse on containers is allowed. Correctness is not in question: 17/17 h2_gate tests pass and every probe equals the H0 clean parse.
